Cardiovascular Microgravity Dynamics

In weightlessness, approximately 1.5 to 2.0 liters of venous fluid shift upward toward the head and thorax (cephalad fluid shift). The baroreceptor reflex downregulates plasma volume by ~10–15% within the first 72 hours. Daily cycle ergometer intervals (CEVIS) and ARED resistance loading maintain left ventricular stroke volume and minimize post-landing orthostatic intolerance.
