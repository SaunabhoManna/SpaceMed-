ⓘWhat is HRV?

Heart rate variability (HRV) is the variation in time between each heartbeat. A higher HRV is often associated with better recovery and resilience. A lower HRV may be associated with fatigue, stress or increased physical load.

☼　Quick tipStay hydrated, get enough sleep and manage stress to support healthy recovery.
